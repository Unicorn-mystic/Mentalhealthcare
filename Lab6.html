Mental Health Care


About Us

Mental Health Care is a web application designed to support emotional well-being by providing access to counselors, wellness assessments, mood tracking, AI-powered guidance and self-care resources.

We believe every individual deserves emotional support, understanding and a safe place to heal. Our platform encourages users to care for both their mind and body through positive habits, self-awareness and professional guidance.
