Mental Health Awareness

Mental health awareness means understanding the importance of our thoughts, feelings, and emotions. It reminds us that taking care of our mind is just as important as taking care of our body.

How Can You Feel Better?

  • Talk to someone you trust.
  • Get enough sleep every day.
  • Eat healthy food and drink enough water.
  • Exercise or go for a short walk.
  • Take breaks from studies or work.
  • Practice deep breathing or meditation.
  • Spend time with your family and friends.

Three Positive Thoughts

  1. Every new day is a chance to start again.
  2. You are stronger than you think.
  3. Small steps every day lead to big changes.

Remember

It is okay to feel sad or stressed sometimes. If you feel overwhelmed, don't hesitate to talk to your parents, teachers, friends, or a mental health professional. Asking for help is a sign of strength.

Take Care of Your Mind. Stay Happy. Stay Healthy.
